# Bundled border-overlay frames

Drop 1000×1500 PNG frame images here to ship them as built-in border presets.

Each PNG should be **1000×1500** with a **transparent center window** — the opaque
frame area becomes the poster's border, and the poster art shows through the
transparent center. They are listed in the Border Replacer's image-overlay picker
as `source: "preset"` and are read-only.

User-uploaded frames live separately in `<config_dir>/border_overlays/` and can be
deleted from the UI; presets here cannot.
