color/: Kometa's default overlay images (github.com/Kometa-Team/Kometa, defaults/overlays/images),
created by Yozora, Bullmoose20 and Sohjiro with base images by Magic815. Each is cropped to its art; the app's
backdrop box (26 px of padding, 305 px minimum width) stands in for Kometa's 305 x 105 box.

white/: the same images with every pixel made white (the transparency is kept).

lettering/: from jmxd's Media Info overlay pack for Kometa (jmxd-overlays, https://buymeacoffee.com/jmxd): the pack's
marks cropped to the lettering, joined with its own dot and spacing. Badges the pack has no mark for are set in the
pack's font (Avenir Next LT Pro Bold) or cut from its marks.
